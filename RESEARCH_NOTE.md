# Research note

## Research question

Can an LLM-driven system move beyond research assistance and operate as a repeatable engine for discovering, formalizing, and empirically validating quantitative alpha?

## System thesis

The bottleneck in factor research is not only computation. It is the repeated conversion of economic ideas into precise, testable objects, followed by disciplined evaluation and retention of useful findings. The implemented system addresses that entire research cycle. It expands hypothesis throughput while keeping empirical measurement deterministic.

This makes the system itself the central research asset. Individual factors are outputs of a capability that can be reused across research domains, extended with new information sets, and compounded through accumulated results.

## Deployment evidence

The first published deployment evaluated an exchange-traded cross section of 257 instruments. One discovered factor was advanced through a point-in-time, chronological walk-forward out-of-sample study covering 2021-01-01 to 2025-08-21.

At a 5 bp one-way cost assumption, the primary portfolio produced 13.83% net CAGR, 1.55 Sharpe, and −4.25% maximum drawdown. A higher-risk expression produced 27.80% net CAGR, 1.52 Sharpe, and −8.47% maximum drawdown. The primary expression remained positive at 10 bp one-way costs and was positive in all five reported calendar buckets.

## Interpretation

The experiment demonstrates successful end-to-end deployment: the system did not merely generate plausible language or a factor description; it produced an executable research output that retained meaningful performance in out-of-sample portfolio evaluation.

The public evidence supports this conclusion at the aggregate level. The methods and implementation required to reconstruct the system remain private.
