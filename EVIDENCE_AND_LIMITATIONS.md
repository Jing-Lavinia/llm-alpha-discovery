# Evidence and limitations

## Evidence status

All reported portfolio figures are historical, walk-forward out-of-sample simulations from 2021-01-01 through 2025-08-21. The study contains 1,123 portfolio observations across a 257-instrument research universe. The statistical sample contains 1,799 qualified cross-sectional observations; its reported HAC t-statistic is 5.27 and its sign is positive in five annual buckets.

## Metric definitions

- **CAGR** is the annualized geometric return over the complete evaluation period.
- **Sharpe** is annualized mean portfolio return divided by annualized volatility, with a zero cash benchmark.
- **Maximum drawdown** is the largest peak-to-trough decline in the simulated portfolio path.
- **Calmar** is CAGR divided by the absolute value of maximum drawdown.
- **ADV participation** compares simulated traded notional with lagged average daily value traded.

## Cost and capacity interpretation

The primary results use a 5 bp one-way transaction-cost assumption. Ten and twenty basis-point scenarios show economic sensitivity rather than alternative fitted strategies. At 10 bp, the primary expression retains an 8.58% CAGR and 1.00 Sharpe; at 20 bp, both expressions lose money.

Capacity figures are scenario diagnostics, not guarantees of executable size. Tail participation rises materially with both capital and portfolio risk, and the maximum is substantially higher than the p95 observation.

## Boundaries

The results are simulated rather than live. Market data and observation-level returns are not redistributed, so the public release supports aggregate evidence review rather than independent reconstruction of the strategy. Reported performance depends on the stated historical sample, modeled frictions, and execution assumptions. The evidence should not be interpreted as investment advice, future performance, or unlimited scalability.
