# Research figures

All nine figures are generated from `evidence/aggregate-evidence.v3.json` or
from the capability-level system diagram in `scripts/build_public_figures.py`.
They share the visual system used across the associated research repositories.

- `research_overview.png` — system identity, research scale, and headline performance.
- `research_context.png` — conceptual progression from isolated factor research to automated R&D.
- `system_architecture.png` — capability map with the discovery engine kept as a black box.
- `research_roles.png` — responsibility split between generative research and deterministic evaluation.
- `evaluation_path.png` — the common empirical path followed by every candidate.
- `performance_profile.png` — return, volatility, drawdown, and Sharpe comparison at 5 bp.
- `cost_sensitivity.png` — net CAGR under 5, 10, and 20 bp one-way costs.
- `annual_consistency.png` — primary-profile returns by calendar bucket.
- `capacity_scenarios.png` — p95 and maximum lagged-ADV participation.

No chart contains factor definitions, security identifiers, signals, positions,
trades, or observation-level returns. PNG metadata is stripped after rendering.
